# ReviewFlow consumer-review policy

- Every rating receives the same flow and the same configured Google destination.
- ReviewFlow never suppresses, delays, intercepts, or diverts negative reviews.
- ReviewFlow never submits a review to Google on a customer’s behalf.
- ReviewFlow never generates a review for someone who was not a customer.
- Feature requests to route customers based on rating are declined as a product policy.

The application helps a customer express their own experience and leaves the final submission entirely to that customer.
